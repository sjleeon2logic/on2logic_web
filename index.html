About On2Logic

ABOUT US

On2Logic is a specialized SoC design company based on the Level-0 business model. We possess in-house tools based on System Verilog, providing a fast and high-quality chip design flow. With numerous mass-production-verified IPs, we realize true Level-0 SoC design and production beyond mere integration. Additionally, by applying IST (In-System Test) technology to SoC, we specialize in SoC design required for MCU, automotive, and aerospace fields. On2Logic aims to grow beyond being the best in Korea to becoming the global No.1 Level-0 company.

Expert

On2Logic is composed of experts from the SoC Industry. Leveraging our strong network with the SoC Industry and in-depth understanding of its systems, we provide exceptional SoC design solutions.

DFT Service

On2logic provides SoC design services that not only incorporate traditional DFT (Design for Test) techniques but also IST (In-System Test) technologies applicable to high-reliability fields such as automotive and aerospace. These advanced methodologies enable us to meet specific customer requirements and deliver highly reliable products.
